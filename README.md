### Hi, I'm Juan Cruz 👋

Senior Frontend Engineer from Argentina (UTC-3). I build React and Next.js frontends for SaaS products, and lately the interfaces that sit on top of AI.

13 years in, six of them with US product teams. Right now I'm at **Globant** on **GlobAI OS**, working on the Agentic Hub, where teams create, configure and run AI agents. Most of my time goes into what the screen does while an agent thinks for 40 seconds, or when a tool call fails halfway through.

**What I'm good at**

- **Frontend architecture that holds up** — feature-first React, a shared component library, a codebase someone new can find their way around.
- **Data-heavy interfaces** — dashboards, tables, maps and reports over real operational data.
- **AI-native UX** — streaming, agent runs, step-by-step progress, approvals, partial failures.
- **End-to-end when it's faster** — Node/NestJS, Prisma, PostgreSQL.
